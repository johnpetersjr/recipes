Sourdough
==========

Ingredients
-----------
* 150g starter
* 250g filterered, warm water
* 25g olive oil
* 500g bread flour
* 10g salt

Optional
--------
* 1 Jalapeno Pepper, diced
* 3oz shredded cheese
* 1 tbsp dried herbs

Steps
-----
1. Mix all ingredients until combined
2. Cover the bowl with wet towel and place in oven with light on for warmth.
3. After 45 minutes, knead the dough well.  Add additional optional ingredients during this knead process.
4. Place back in oven.  Stretch and fold the dough twice more after 45 minutes twice.
5. Sprinkle cast iron pot with cornmeal.  Place dough in pot on counter and cover for one hour to rise after the final stretch/fold.  Preheat oven to 400.
6. Score the top of the dough and bake with lid on for 20 minutes. Remove lid and continue baking for 35 minutes, or until internal bread temperature is 208. 
8. Remove from pot and cool on wire rack immediately.
