Lentil Stew (Vegetarian)
=======================================
Delicious, healthy, low calorie, and EASY

Ingredients
-----------
* 1 cup dry lentils
* 3.5 cups broth / stock
* 1 carrot, large diced
* 2 sticks celery, sliced
* 2 medium potatoes, diced in medium cubes (small enough to be eaten in a single spoonful)
* 1 onion, large diced
* 1 14.5oz can diced tomatoes
* Garlic Cloves (2-3)
* 2 tbsp Za'tar seasoning
* 1 tbsp parsley
* Salt & Pepper

Steps
-----
1. Rinse and drain the lentils.
2. Add all ingredients to big pot, cover and simmer for 50 minutes, stirring occasionally.
